Field Warfare

Privacy policy · Last updated November 11, 2026

SupportPrivacy

Field Warfare does not collect any personal data. The game has no account, no advertising, no usage analytics, no trackers and no third-party services, and sends nothing to its developer.

Data stored on your device

The game keeps only your player name, settings, custom controls and personal records, and only on your device. This data never leaves the device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Camera and sensors

  • Camera (augmented reality): used only when you pick an augmented-reality view, to place the battlefield on a table or in your room. Camera images and the room scan (including LiDAR) are processed live on the device by ARKit; they are neither saved nor transmitted.
  • Gyroscope: used, if you turn it on, to aim by moving the device. Readings stay on the device.

Local network and online play

  • The app uses Bonjour to find games on your local network. When you host a game, it announces it on your local network with your player name.
  • When you join or host a game, your player name, team and game actions (movement, shots) are exchanged with that game's server and shown to the other players. You choose that server: your device, a computer on your network or an address you enter. The developer runs no game servers and does not receive this information.
  • The game's built-in server keeps this information in memory for the duration of the match only and does not store it.

Children

Since no data is collected, no data from children is collected either.
