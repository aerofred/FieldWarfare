Frequently asked questions

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 15 or later. The game is played in landscape; turning the device to portrait pauses it.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Solo play against computer-controlled bots works fully offline.

How do I play with friends?

Multiplayer works on a local network. Every player must be on the same Wi-Fi network. One player hosts the match on their device, and the others pick that match in the list of games found on the network. Empty slots are filled by bots.

Other players can't see my game

  • Check that every device is on the same Wi-Fi network. Some guest or public networks block devices from seeing each other.
  • Check that Field Warfare has local network access: Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network.
  • Keep the hosting app in the foreground: on iOS, a hosted match stops when the app goes to the background.

Augmented reality doesn't start

  • Check that Field Warfare has camera access: Settings › Privacy & Security › Camera.
  • Tabletop and room views need an ARKit device. Full-room scanning with furniture needs a LiDAR device (iPad Pro, iPhone Pro); other devices detect the floor and walls only.
  • Scan slowly in a well-lit room. Plain or reflective surfaces are harder to detect.

The game has no sound

On iPhone, the silent switch mutes the game. Check it and the volume.

Can I use a game controller?

Yes. MFi, Xbox and PlayStation controllers are supported. On-screen controls hide while a controller is in use.
